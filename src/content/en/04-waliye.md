## What is Massar Waliye?

Massar Waliye is the space intended for parents and guardians within the Massar system. It allows a parent or guardian to follow the school information made available for their child, such as grades, attendance and some records connected with the school pathway, depending on permissions and what the school publishes.

The space does not give a parent the power to change school decisions. It is a monitoring and communication tool, not a replacement for the school.

## Who uses Waliye?

It is used by a father, mother or legal guardian, according to the details recognised by the school. The account may need to be linked to the student, and the process can differ between schools. Your school is the first source of information.

If you are using the student's account instead of your own, you may see different information or miss features. Ask the school which space is appropriate for your situation.

## Preparing the account

1. Check the documents provided by the school. Do they refer to Waliye or Moutamadris?
2. Prepare your own login details, not your child's.
3. Confirm that the account is linked to the correct student.
4. Open the official address from this page or the Ministry website.
5. If you have more than one child, check how to switch between accounts if that option is available.

> [!OFFICIAL] Your child's privacy
> School information is personal data. Do not publish it in public groups or send it to websites offering to "improve grades" or "extract results". Use only the official platform.

## What you may be able to follow

Available items vary by school and account. They may include:

- Grades by subject and period.
- Attendance and lateness.
- Information about the class and school pathway.
- Some administrative notices or messages.

Do not assume that every item will always be available. Schools may publish data in stages, and some subjects may be delayed. If something is missing, it does not automatically mean the account is broken.

## Following progress without adding pressure

Digital monitoring is a tool, not a final judgment on a student. A single grade may be partial or linked to one test. It helps to:

- Discuss results calmly rather than comparing them with classmates.
- Ask the teacher when a gap is unclear, not an unofficial website.
- Follow attendance early, because small problems can grow.
- Keep login details safe and share them only with people who genuinely need access.

## If you cannot sign in

Check:

- That you selected Waliye, not Moutamadris.
- That the username and password are typed correctly.
- Your keyboard language and Caps Lock.
- That your browser is updated, or try another browser.
- Whether there is a temporary service notice.

If the problem continues, contact the school. Do not pay someone who promises to recover the account, and do not send identity documents or your child's details to an unknown person.

## Security and responsibility

- Never share your password or one-time code.
- Avoid unofficial "checker" or "accelerator" tools.
- Do not save the password on a shared device.
- If you notice unusual activity, change the password through the official channel and inform the school.

## Summary

Waliye is useful for monitoring, but it does not replace communication with the school. Use the correct account, respect your child's privacy, and contact the school when something is wrong or missing.
