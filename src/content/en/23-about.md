## Why we built this site

Massar services are numerous, but the information about them is scattered across posts, group chats and isolated replies. Much of it is old or inaccurate, and some of it asks for personal data in exchange for "help" that never arrives.

We built Massar Services to gather a clear explanation in one place: what the service is, who uses it, what to do next, and when to stop and contact the school. We do not aim to replace the official body, but to help you reach it with confidence.

## Our independence

We are **not** the Ministry of National Education, not a school, and not an official platform. We have no authority to create or activate an account, change a grade, issue a result or decide an administrative request.

That independence matters for two reasons:

1. We do not claim authority we do not have.
2. We can explain the limits of information instead of hiding them.

When we link to an official page, we are pointing you to it as a source, not because we are part of it.

## What we offer

- Clear explainers on Massar services.
- Practical guides for students, parents and teaching staff.
- Explanations of the difference between grades and results, and between the different spaces.
- Security warnings about phishing and data sharing.
- Links to official sources for verification.
- Published editorial, corrections and accessibility policies.

## What we do not offer

- We do not create or recover Massar accounts.
- We do not display official grades or results.
- We do not change data in the system.
- We do not provide legal advice or administrative decisions.
- We do not guarantee admission, a result or compensation.
- We do not ask for your password or Massar code.

## Our approach

We start from the reader's question, then look for the most appropriate source. For sensitive information we prefer the official source. If we cannot find a current source, we say so instead of filling the gap with a guess.

We review pages periodically and update the last-modified date. We follow a clear corrections policy when an error is established. We do not publish exam dates, holidays or results that we have not verified.

> [!NOTE] Transparency before perfection
> A page may contain incomplete information. It is better to admit the limit and point you to the official source than to offer false certainty.

## The editorial team

The site is produced collectively under the name "Massar Services editorial team". We do not attribute articles to invented people, and we do not display qualifications or titles that are not real. When specialist review is needed, we say so clearly.

## How to reach us

You can report an error, a broken link or outdated information through the [contact page](/en/contact-us/). Do not send your password, account details or unnecessary personal documents. See the [corrections policy](/en/corrections-policy/) to learn how we handle reports.

## Summary

Massar Services is an independent site that explains rather than administers. We gather information, name our sources, admit the limits and point you to the official body. If that is what you need, you are in the right place.
