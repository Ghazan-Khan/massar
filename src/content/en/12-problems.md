## Before you start

Define the problem precisely. "Massar does not work" can mean several different things:

- I cannot open the website.
- I can open it, but login fails.
- I signed in, but I cannot see my grades.
- I see incorrect information.
- A specific error message appears.

Each case has a different diagnosis. Write down exactly what happens, and do not start by changing your password or sharing your account.

## Quick diagnosis

| Symptom | Common possibility | First step |
|---|---|---|
| Page will not open | Connection or temporary outage | Try another network and browser |
| Username or password error | Wrong details or wrong space | Check the space and your typing |
| Account not activated | Administrative issue | Contact the school |
| Grades missing | Not published or not updated | Check the period and account |
| Slow website | Load or weak connection | Wait and try later |
| Permission message | Account not authorised | Contact the relevant administrator |

## Login problems

### Wrong details

Check:

- The correct username, not an email address if that is different.
- Your keyboard language.
- Caps Lock.
- No leading or trailing space.
- That you are not using another person's account.

### Wrong space

A parent may need Waliye rather than Moutamadris. An education professional may need a different portal. Check the documents provided by the school.

### Inactive account

A new account may need activation by the school. Do not create a parallel account without guidance, as this can cause duplicate records.

## Grade and record problems

- **Missing grade:** the subject may not be assessed or published yet.
- **Different grade:** check the period and assessment type, then contact the school.
- **Unexpected absence:** check the account, then ask the school.
- **Old information:** refresh the page or wait for the data to update.

This website cannot edit any record. A site claiming otherwise is not trustworthy.

## Technical problems

Try, in order:

1. Refresh the page without resubmitting a form.
2. Clear the browser cache or use a private window.
3. Disable suspicious extensions.
4. Try another browser.
5. Try another network.
6. Check that your device is not using an untrusted intermediary app or VPN.

If the problem affects many users, it is likely a service issue rather than your account.

> [!WARNING] Do not trust quick fixes
> Do not pay someone who promises to unlock your account or reveal grades, and do not send them your password or one-time code. Real solutions go through official channels.

## When to contact the school

Contact the school or relevant administration if:

- Login still fails after your checks.
- There is a problem with activation or permissions.
- Grades or attendance are incorrect.
- You never received login details.
- You notice unusual account activity.

Provide clear information: your role, the space, the error text and what you have tried. Do not send your password.

## Summary

Identify the problem and the space, check the details, address and connection, wait if it is a publishing issue, then contact the school. Avoid middlemen and never share your credentials.
