## The site's independence

Massar Services is an independent information site. It **does not represent** the Ministry of National Education or any school or government body, and there is no official connection, delegation or partnership between them.

Any reference to "Massar", "the ministry" or platform names is a descriptive reference to existing services. It does not mean that we own or operate them.

## The content is informational

The published content is intended for explanation and general guidance. It is not:

- Legal or administrative advice.
- An official decision.
- An approved document.
- A substitute for the school's instructions.
- A guarantee of admission, a result or compensation.

Pages may contain general information that does not apply to every case. Procedures, links or conditions may change without notice.

## No guarantee of accuracy or completeness

We make a reasonable effort to verify information and name our sources. However, we do not guarantee:

- That every statement is accurate at all times.
- That every official link works from every location.
- That the content covers every exception.
- That a described procedure is still in force.

Where the site's content conflicts with the official source, the official source prevails.

## Results and accounts

We do not provide account services, and we cannot:

- Access your account.
- Recover a password.
- Display grades or results.
- Change data.
- Influence an administrative decision.

Do not share your password, Massar code or verification code with anyone. We are not responsible for any harm resulting from sharing your details with another party.

## External links

We may link to official sites or references. We do not control their content, security or policies, and we do not guarantee that they remain available. Visiting an external link is at your own risk.

## Lawful use

The site must be used in accordance with these terms and applicable law. It may not be used for unlawful, misleading or harmful activity.

## Limits of liability

To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for any direct or indirect loss resulting from:

- Reliance on unverified content.
- Site downtime or a technical error.
- Content on an external site.
- An administrative or educational decision taken by another body.
- Misuse of login details by the user or anyone else.

This clause does not exclude any liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## Corrections

If you find an error, report it through the [contact page](/en/contact-us/). We review the report under the [corrections policy](/en/corrections-policy/) and correct the page once the error is established.

## Summary

Use the site as a starting point for understanding, not as the end of verification. For official and sensitive information, return to the ministry, the school or the official platform. We help you ask the right question, not bypass the competent body.
