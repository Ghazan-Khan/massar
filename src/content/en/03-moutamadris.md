## What is Massar Moutamadris?

Massar Moutamadris is the digital space intended for students within the Massar system. It lets a student view academic information published by their school, such as records connected with their school pathway, grades and attendance, according to what is available in their account.

The space does not replace the school. If a record or decision is wrong, it cannot be corrected through this website. Corrections go through the school or the relevant administration, following official procedures.

## Before you sign in

Prepare three things:

1. **Your login details** as provided by the school. Do not assume that Waliye or Moudaris credentials will work here.
2. **The official address** for Moutamadris. Check that the domain is `massarservice.men.gov.ma`.
3. **An updated browser**, preferably on a private connection rather than an open public network.

If you do not have login details, do not guess repeatedly. Contact the school. Repeated attempts may lock the account or delay the solution.

## Sign-in steps

1. Open the official Moutamadris address from this page or from the Ministry website.
2. Enter the username and password provided by your school, checking your keyboard language.
3. Make sure there are no extra spaces at the beginning or end of a field.
4. Complete any verification step that appears, and never share the code.
5. After signing in, look for the section related to grades, academic information or attendance.

If an error message appears, write it down exactly. The wording can help the school distinguish between wrong details, an inactive account and a temporary service problem.

> [!SECURITY] Check the address
> Do not enter your password on a page reached through a shortened link, a text message or an advertisement. Type the official address yourself or use a link from the Ministry website.

## After you sign in

Do not rush to interpret every number. Grades may be provisional or partial, and they may be updated after marking or data entry. Check:

- The school and class, to confirm you are in the right account.
- Grades by subject and period, where available.
- Attendance or lateness records, if shown.
- Any notice or message published by the school.

If something differs from what you expected, do not change anything yourself and do not share the account with a classmate. Collect the details and contact the school.

## When grades or records are missing

Possible reasons include:

- The school has not published the grade yet.
- The grade is still being reviewed or entered.
- You are using the wrong account.
- The system is temporarily updating.
- The information appears in another space, such as Waliye, rather than Moutamadris.

Confirm the account and address, wait a reasonable period, then contact the school. Do not use "grade checker" websites that ask for your username and password.

## Account security

- Never share your password with a friend, relative or another website.
- Do not post screenshots containing personal information in public groups.
- Avoid simple passwords or passwords reused from other accounts.
- If you suspect someone used your account, change the password through the official channel and tell the school.

## Summary

Moutamadris is a viewing and monitoring tool, not a correction office. Use the official address, keep your details private, and contact the school when something is missing or you cannot sign in.
