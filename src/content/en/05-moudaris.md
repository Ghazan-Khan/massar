## What is Massar Moudaris?

Massar Moudaris is the professional space for teachers and administrative staff within the Massar system. It differs from the student and parent spaces in purpose and permissions. A teacher or administrator may use it to access records connected with their work, such as classes, grades, attendance or other tasks, depending on what their account allows.

There is no single view for every professional. Permissions shape what appears. What you see may differ from a colleague's view, and that is not necessarily an error.

## The difference between the spaces

| Space | User | Nature |
|---|---|---|
| Moutamadris | Student | View their academic information |
| Waliye | Parent or guardian | Follow available information about their child |
| Moudaris | Teachers and administrative staff | Work with records according to permissions |

If you try to enter Moudaris with a student or parent account, the attempt will normally fail. Start by identifying the account type.

## Accessing the space

The entry point and redirection can vary by school and role. Therefore:

1. Use the official address provided by your administration or school.
2. Check the domain before entering credentials.
3. Do not use a link from an unknown message or unofficial group.
4. If you are unsure which portal applies, ask your administrative or technical contact.

This website does not provide accounts and cannot fix permissions. We do not ask education professionals to send passwords or screenshots of the system.

## Permissions and responsibilities

Access is tied to a role and a task, not to seniority or personal preference. Options may differ between:

- A subject teacher.
- A lead or coordinating teacher.
- An administrative staff member.
- A technical administrator or headteacher.

If you need additional access, the request goes to the competent person in your school or administration, not to this website.

> [!SECURITY] Sensitive professional data
> Student records are personal data. Do not share them outside the scope of your work, store them on unapproved cloud services, or send screenshots through unofficial apps.

## Common access problems

- **Account inactive or suspended:** contact the administrative contact.
- **Wrong password:** check the keyboard and use the official recovery route if available.
- **Missing permission:** ask the competent authority; do not use a colleague's account.
- **Service not responding:** check your connection and any official notice, then inform your technical contact.
- **Interface changed:** the system may have been updated. Do not rely on old instructions without checking.

## Professional account security

- Use a strong, unique password.
- Do not share the account with colleagues or trainees.
- Sign out on shared devices.
- Do not open suspicious links claiming to update Massar data.
- Report unusual access immediately.

## Summary

Moudaris is a professional space governed by permissions. Use the official portal provided by your school, respect the confidentiality of student data, and contact the responsible person when an account or permission problem arises.
