## How to use this library

The guides are arranged so you can move from general understanding to practical action. If Massar is new to you, start with an overview, then choose the space that fits you. If you have a specific problem, go straight to the relevant help guide.

We do not publish explainers built on unconfirmed dates or results. Where information can change, we say so and point you to the official body.

> [!TIP] Start with one question
> Do not try to read every guide at once. Write your question in one sentence, then choose the closest guide. That is faster than browsing at random.

## Two ways in

**I need help with an account or a procedure.** Start with the Massar guides: what the platform is, which space applies to you, how to sign in, how to recover a password, and how to read grades and results.

**I want to understand the system.** Start with the learning guides: orientation, the structure of Moroccan education, the school calendar, official platforms and student resources.

## A note on dates and results

Education information changes during the year. A guide may describe a process correctly and still be out of date on a date. Always check the publication and update dates at the top of a page, and confirm anything official with the ministry, the academy or the school.

We never publish an exam date, holiday or result that we have not verified against an official source. If a page does not give a date, that is usually deliberate.

## If a guide does not answer your question

- Use the search page with a short phrase, not a full sentence.
- Check the FAQ, which collects the most common questions.
- Contact us with the page address and what you were trying to do.

We cannot access your account or act on your behalf, but a precise question helps us improve the guide for the next reader.
