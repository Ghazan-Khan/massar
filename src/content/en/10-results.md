## Grades are not results

In everyday use, many people confuse "grades" and "results". Grades are assessment records that may be ongoing. A result is a conclusion or decision linked to an exam, progression or certificate. Each has a different publishing route.

A grade may appear in Massar before a final result is announced, and a result may be announced officially before it becomes visible in every space. Do not draw conclusions from a simple comparison.

## Where results are usually announced

The channel depends on the type of result and the level of study. Possible channels include:

- An official announcement from the Ministry of National Education or a regional academy.
- A notice from the school.
- An official digital space dedicated to that result.
- Official lists or documents handed to the student or guardian.

These are general channels, not a guarantee that every result appears everywhere. Rely on the official announcement for the specific result you are waiting for.

## How to verify a result

1. **Identify the type:** a subject grade, a term average, an exam result or an orientation decision.
2. **Find the official announcement:** the Ministry, school or academy website.
3. **Check the date and source:** a screenshot with no link or date is not proof.
4. **Use the official channel directly:** not a shortened link or paid advertisement.
5. **Keep the official document:** a screen alone may not be enough for some procedures.

## Why a result may be delayed

- Marking or review is still in progress.
- Data is being entered and checked.
- Administrative steps must be completed before publication.
- The platform is under pressure when many users connect at once.
- Publication timing differs between schools or authorities.

A delay does not automatically mean your account is broken. If it goes beyond the usual period, ask the school or the announcing authority.

## Warning signs

Be careful with:

- Websites asking for your Massar password in exchange for a result.
- Copied result screens with no source or an old date.
- Messages asking for payment to "speed up" a result.
- Links sent in groups that request student details.
- Unofficial apps requesting excessive permissions on your phone.

> [!SECURITY] Nobody needs your password to announce a result
> An official authority does not need your password to publish a result. If someone asks for it, they are not the right channel.

## If the result is unexpected

Do not start by publishing it or attacking the school. First check:

- Does the result belong to the right student?
- Is it from the current session or a previous year?
- Is there a note about an appeal or review?
- Is there an official document explaining the calculation?

Then contact the school or the competent authority through official channels, and respect any deadlines for appeals or review.

## Summary

Massar results are part of a wider system, and not every result appears in one place. Verify the official source, avoid copied screens and middlemen, and contact the school when there is a delay or a discrepancy.
