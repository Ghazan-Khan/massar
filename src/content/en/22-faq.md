faq: Is Massar Services an official site?|No. It is an independent information site, not affiliated with the Ministry of National Education or any government body. We explain and point you to the official platforms.
faq: What is the difference between Massar Moutamadris, Waliye and Moudaris?|Moutamadris is the student space, Waliye is the parent or guardian space, and Moudaris is the space for teaching and administrative staff. Each has a different account and permissions.
faq: Where do I find Massar grades?|Usually in the space linked to your account: Moutamadris for a student or Waliye for a parent, once the school has published them. Grades do not necessarily appear as soon as they are entered.
faq: Why are grades or results missing?|The school may not have published them yet, the data may be updating, or you may be in the wrong space. Check the account and the period, then contact the school if it continues.
faq: Can you recover my password?|No. We cannot access Massar accounts and have no authority to change passwords. Use the official recovery process if available, or contact the school.
faq: Is the Massar code the same as the password?|No. The Massar code identifies the student, while the password is the secret used to sign in. Do not share either with people or unofficial sites.
faq: How do I get a Massar code?|Usually from the school or from official documents. Do not trust sites that "generate" codes or intermediaries asking for your details.
faq: Can I sign in to Massar from my phone?|You can try using your phone browser to reach the official site. Avoid untrusted public networks, and check the address before entering a password.
faq: What should I do if I see an error message?|Write down the message, and check the space, the link and your details. Do not retry dozens of times. If the problem continues, contact the school.
faq: Do you offer technical support or accounts?|No. We provide information only. We do not create or activate accounts and we do not change grades or results.
faq: Why do you not publish exam and holiday dates?|Because we do not publish dates we have not verified with an official source. Dates can change and vary between regions, and publishing them unverified causes harm.
faq: Where can I find baccalaureate results?|They are announced through the official channels set by the ministry or the competent authorities, and not necessarily in every Massar space. See the baccalaureate results guide.
faq: Is the site safe for reading guides?|Yes. You do not need to enter account details to read the content. We do not create accounts on this site, and no page asks for your Massar password.
faq: How do I report an error or a broken link?|Use the contact page and name the page, the statement and the link. Do not send your password or account details. See the corrections policy for the process.
faq: Can I republish content from this site?|You may share links. Full or commercial republication requires permission under the terms of use.
faq: Is the site available in English?|Yes. Use the language button at the top of the page. Some pages are fully translated, and editorial details may differ slightly between the two languages.
faq: Do you use advertising or sell my data?|We do not sell your data. See the privacy policy to learn what is collected when you use the contact form or if analytics are enabled.
faq: Are there legal pages?|Yes. The privacy policy, terms of use, disclaimer, editorial policy, corrections policy and accessibility statement are linked at the bottom of every page.
faq: How do I contact the editorial team?|Through the contact page or the email address listed there. Do not send login details or unnecessary personal documents.
faq: Can I rely on this site for an administrative decision?|No. The content is informational and does not replace the school or the official body. Administrative decisions and official results are issued by the competent authorities.

## How to use this page

Find your question in the list, then open it to read the answer. If your question concerns an official procedure or a personal account, start with the linked guide, then contact the school where needed.

These answers are general. Details may differ by school, school year or account type. We do not guarantee that every process described here is available in every case.

> [!NOTE] We are an independent site
> We do not represent the Ministry of National Education, and we cannot access your account or change your data. Our aim is to help you understand the service and reach the official channel.
