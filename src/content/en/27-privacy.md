## Who is responsible for processing?

Massar Services is run by an independent editorial team. For the purposes of this policy, data questions can be sent to the email address listed on the contact page: contact@massarservices.ma.

This policy covers this site only. The official sites we link to have their own policies, which we do not control.

## What data may we collect?

### Data you send yourself

When you use the contact form, we may collect:

- The name you enter.
- Your email address.
- The subject of the message.
- The page or link concerned.
- The text of the message.
- The message language and date.

Messages sent through the form are saved in a private database on the site's own server so that we can follow them up and keep a record of corrections. That database is not reachable from the web and only the editorial team can read it. Messages are also sent by email to the address on the contact page.

We do not ask for a password, a Massar code or account details. You should not send personal documents or sensitive information we do not need.

### Technical data

The hosting provider may record basic technical information, such as IP address, browser type and request time, for security and service operation. We do not use this data to identify you or to build a marketing profile.

### Cookies

The site currently uses no analytics or advertising cookies. Fonts and core files are hosted locally. If an analytics tool is enabled in future, we will update this policy and explain the tool, its purpose and how to object where applicable.

## Why do we process data?

- To reply to your message or correct content.
- To improve the quality and security of the site.
- To comply with a legal obligation where one exists.
- To prevent misuse of the form or spam.

The basis for processing contact form data is your consent when you send it, together with our legitimate interest in improving content and security.

## Who can see it?

Messages reach the editorial team, and may be seen by the hosting or email provider as a technical processor. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for marketing purposes.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law, or to protect our rights or the safety of users.

## How long is it kept?

We keep contact messages in the database for up to **12 months** from the date you send them, unless we still need them to handle an open request or to comply with a legal obligation, after which they are deleted automatically. You can ask us to delete a message sooner using the contact page. The hosting provider may keep technical logs for a limited period under its own policies.

## Where is data processed?

Hosting or email processing may take place outside Morocco. In that case we aim to choose providers that offer reasonable safeguards, and we limit processing to what the service needs.

## Your rights

Under Moroccan law on the protection of personal data, and to the extent it applies, you may:

- Request access to your data.
- Request correction of inaccurate information.
- Request deletion where there is no legitimate reason to keep it.
- Object to a particular processing operation.
- Withdraw your consent to processing a message, without retroactive effect on earlier processing.

To make a request, write to the address above and explain what you need. We may ask for enough information to verify your identity before acting.

## Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect messages, such as restricting access and using encrypted connections. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Do not send sensitive data we do not need.

## Children

The site is aimed at a general education audience, but the contact form is not intended for children. If a user is a minor, they should make contact through a parent or guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal data about children through the form.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the service, a tool or the legal framework changes. The last update date appears at the top of the page. Continuing to use the site after an update means you have seen the new version.

## Contact

For any privacy question or request, use the [contact page](/en/contact-us/) or the email address listed there. Do not send your password or account details.
