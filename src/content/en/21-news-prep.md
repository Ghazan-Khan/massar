## Start with the basic paperwork

Before talking about notebooks and routines, confirm the administrative side:

- Is registration complete at the correct school?
- Are any documents missing?
- Has the class or school changed?
- Do you need more information from the administration?
- Is the Massar account linked to the student correct and active?

If there is a problem with registration or the account, deal with it first. Fixing administrative problems after lessons begin is harder.

## Confirming the start date

The **2026-2027 school year began on 7 September 2026**, according to the official announcement from the Ministry of National Education. That date is confirmed. Holiday and exam dates still require checking against official announcements.

Do not rely on tables circulating in group chats. See the [school calendar guide](/en/school-calendar/) to learn how to check.

## Preparing digital accounts

- Confirm the right space: Massar Moutamadris for the student, Massar Waliye for the parent or guardian.
- Do not share the password with anyone.
- Make sure the account is linked to the correct student.
- Try signing in before you urgently need grades.
- If the data does not work, contact the school early.

Do not pay an intermediary to "fix the account". Do not send your details to a site promising a faster service.

## Organising routine

Going back to school is not a moment but a gradual transition:

1. Adjust sleep times a few days before the start.
2. Set aside a quiet study place if possible.
3. Prepare a realistic weekly schedule.
4. Keep time for rest and movement.
5. Agree with the student on how homework will be followed up.
6. Avoid an ideal timetable that cannot be kept.

## Contacting the school

Ask about:

- The class and the main teacher.
- Arrival and departure times.
- Required documents.
- The official way to communicate.
- Activity or meeting days.
- Absence and lateness procedures.

Keep official phone numbers and channels, and avoid relying on information passed on verbally.

## What you do not need

- Unconfirmed holiday dates.
- "Guaranteed summaries" sold as official.
- Unknown apps that ask for account details.
- Groups that ask for photos of a student's documents.
- Promises of results or guaranteed progression.

## After the start

Review after the first week:

- Did the student attend every lesson?
- Is there a subject that needs support?
- Does the digital account work?
- Has any message arrived from the school?
- Is the routine working, or does it need adjusting?

Early follow-up is better than waiting until the exam period.

> [!TIP] A simple rule
> If the information concerns an official date or procedure, check the official source. If it concerns daily study, start with the teacher and the school.

## Summary

Good preparation starts with paperwork and the right account, then routine and communication. Use the official start date, and do not build plans on unconfirmed dates. The checklist can be adapted to each family's circumstances.
