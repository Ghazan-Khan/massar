## What is confirmed

According to the Ministry of National Education's official announcement, the **2026-2027 school year began on 7 September 2026**. We verified this from the Ministry's official page, and it is the only date we confirm here.

We do not add other dates simply because they are circulating. Holiday and exam dates can change, may differ between regions, and should not be presented as fact without a source.

## Why we do not publish a full calendar

The school calendar contains details that change every year:

- Mid-term break dates.
- Public and religious holiday breaks.
- Exam periods.
- Exceptional closure days.
- Regional or school-level differences.

Publishing an unverified calendar can cause mistakes in travel, family arrangements or exam preparation. It is better to rely on the official announcement.

## Types of school breaks

The Moroccan school calendar usually includes types of breaks such as:

- Autumn break.
- Mid-year break.
- Spring break.
- Summer holiday.
- Breaks linked to national or religious occasions.

These are general categories, not dates. Each year has its own arrangements.

## How to verify a date

1. Open the Ministry of National Education website.
2. Look for the announcement or calendar for the current school year.
3. Check the publication date, not the date it was shared on social media.
4. Check your regional academy or school notice if the date concerns your area.
5. Ask the school before booking travel or making family plans.

> [!WARNING] Beware of copied dates
> Old or edited calendars may circulate as the "official calendar". Check the school year and the publisher before relying on them.

## What this means for students and families

- Plan breaks only after official confirmation.
- Follow school notices about exams and tests.
- Do not assume every region has the same dates.
- Keep some flexibility in family arrangements.
- Review the calendar at the start of each term.

## Exams

Exam dates are not always part of the same general calendar. Competent authorities or schools may announce them separately. Do not rely on dates circulating in chat groups, especially during reform years.

## Summary

The confirmed information here is that the 2026-2027 school year began on 7 September 2026. For other dates, check with the Ministry or the competent authority. We do not publish rumours or guarantee dates that have not been officially announced.
