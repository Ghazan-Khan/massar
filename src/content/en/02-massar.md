## What is Massar?

Massar is the digital environment connected with school education in Morocco. It links schools and education administrations with student records. It is used to manage information such as enrolment, the academic pathway, grades, attendance, results and orientation, within the permissions attached to each user.

The important distinction is between **the system** and **the space you sign in to**. The underlying system may be shared, but the interfaces are not. A student normally uses a different space from a parent or a teacher. Choosing the wrong space is one of the most common reasons a login attempt fails.

## The main spaces

| Space | Who uses it | General purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Massar Moutamadris | Student | View academic information published by the school |
| Massar Waliye | Parent or guardian | Follow available school information about their child |
| Massar Moudaris | Teachers and administrative staff | Work with records according to permissions |

This is a general map. What appears in an account can vary by school, role and publishing stage. Do not assume that everything a classmate sees will also appear in your account.

> [!OFFICIAL] Official links
> Account actions and official results are handled on Ministry platforms. Check the address before entering credentials, and never sign in through a link sent by an unknown person.

## What can you find in Massar?

Massar typically holds information related to a student's school pathway: enrolment, class, grades, attendance and some administrative or educational records. Results or orientation decisions may appear in specific spaces or at specific stages, depending on what the responsible authority publishes.

Massar is not a general help desk. If your question concerns a school decision, a stream change or how a grade was calculated, the right source is your school or the relevant education administration, not an external website.

## The limits of this guide

We rely on general information and publicly available official links. We do not publish exam dates, holiday dates or results unless we have verified them through an official source. When information changes frequently, such as grade publishing or enrolment procedures, we say so instead of presenting an unverified date.

> [!WARNING] Never share login details
> No legitimate support team needs your password or one-time code. Anyone who asks for them to "fix" your account or "reveal" your grades is putting your account at risk.

## How to choose the right space

1. Identify your role: student, parent or guardian, or education professional.
2. Check the document or message provided by the school, because it usually names the correct account and address.
3. Go to the official website directly, not through an advertisement or a shortened link.
4. If sign-in fails, read the login guide before trying random passwords.
5. If the problem continues, contact the school or the relevant administration.

## Security comes first

Your Massar Code, password and one-time codes are private. Do not post them in chat groups, comments or forms on unofficial websites. Even when asking for help, ask someone to explain the next step rather than handing over your credentials.

## Summary

Massar is one system with several user spaces. Choose the space that matches your role, use the official address, and contact the school when something does not work. This guide helps you understand the picture; the official procedure remains with the competent authority.
