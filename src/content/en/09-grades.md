## What are Massar grades?

Massar grades are assessment records linked to a student and displayed in the digital space after the school enters and publishes them. They may include tests, activities and continuous assessment, and the display can differ between schools.

It helps to distinguish between:

- **An ongoing grade:** measures learning or an activity in a specific period and may be adjusted.
- **A final subject grade:** calculated according to school and system rules.
- **A final result:** a decision or average connected with progression or an exam, with a different publishing and verification process.

## Where do grades come from?

The Massar platform does not create grades automatically. The source is the teacher and the school: tests are taken, marked, entered and then published according to internal procedures. A delay between a test and its appearance is therefore normal.

Some grades may also be hidden from a student or parent for a period, depending on school settings and publishing policy. That does not automatically mean something is broken.

## Why grades differ between accounts

Visible information may vary because of:

- Different permissions between a student and a parent account.
- Different schools, classes or subjects.
- Publishing timing and data updates.
- A grade still under review or correction.
- The selected academic period.

Do not compare your account with a classmate's and conclude that yours is broken. Check the period, subject and space first.

## Reading grades usefully

1. Confirm the school and class to ensure you are in the right account.
2. Select the academic period you want to review.
3. Read the subject and assessment type, not just the number.
4. Note whether the grade is ongoing or final.
5. Write down questions instead of relying on memory.
6. Discuss unclear results with the teacher or school.

## If a grade is missing

Work through this sequence:

1. Confirm you are using the correct account.
2. Check that the period shown is the one you need.
3. Wait a reasonable period, especially after tests.
4. Contact the school if several subjects are missing or the gap persists.
5. Do not send your details to "grade checker" websites.

> [!NOTE] A delay is not proof of an error
> Grades may be delayed by marking, data entry or review. Do not assume that your account was hacked or your result was lost simply because a grade is late.

## If a grade is wrong

A grade cannot be corrected from this website. Before contacting the school, collect:

- The subject and period.
- The grade shown and the grade you expected.
- The assessment type, if known.
- The date you noticed the issue.
- A screenshot or evidence if permitted, with unnecessary personal details hidden.

Avoid public arguments in chat groups. The correct procedure starts with the school.

## Privacy

Grades are personal data. Do not publish them in public groups, and do not share your account with friends or websites asking for your password. Even when seeking help, describe the problem without revealing login details.

## Summary

Massar grades are a monitoring tool, not a final judgment at every stage. Understand the type of grade and when it is published, check the account and period, and contact the school when needed. Never share credentials or pay for "grade correction".
