## An overview

School education in Morocco is generally organised in successive stages: preschool, primary, middle school, and upper secondary education ending with the baccalaureate. After that come university study, vocational training or other pathways.

This is a general structure. Terminology, durations and pathways may vary with reforms, regions and institutions. This page is therefore an initial map, not a legal reference.

## The stages

| Stage | Typical duration | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Preschool | Varies | Not necessarily compulsory; provision is expanding |
| Primary | 6 years | Normally begins at age six |
| Middle school | 3 years | Followed by secondary education |
| Upper secondary | 3 years | Ends with the baccalaureate |
| Higher education and training | Varies | University and vocational pathways |

Typical durations do not mean every student follows the same pace. Repetition, a change of pathway or orientation can occur.

## Where Massar fits

It is part of the administrative and educational structure, not a separate stage of education.

## The baccalaureate and beyond

The baccalaureate is the qualification at the end of upper secondary education. It opens several possibilities, including:

- University study.
- Preparatory classes or selective pathways.
- Vocational training.
- Certain recognised private pathways.

Each pathway has its own conditions, thresholds and deadlines. Do not rely on old general information; check the official announcements of the institutions concerned.

## Preschool

Preschool is an important stage in a child's development, but its organisation, duration and enrolment arrangements vary by institution and region. Do not assume it is compulsory in the same way in every case. Ask the local school about the current position.

## Reforms and change

Morocco's education system has seen multiple reforms, and pathways, terminology or progression conditions may change. Therefore:

- Check the date of any article you read.
- Consult the official Ministry website.
- Ask the school about the current school year.
- Do not use an old certificate to describe the current system.

> [!NOTE] This is an introductory page
> It does not provide binding legal or administrative detail. Its purpose is to help you understand the terminology before checking the official source.
