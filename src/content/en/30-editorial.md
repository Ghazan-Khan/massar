## Our editorial aim

We aim to provide a clear and useful explanation of Massar services and education in Morocco, helping the reader understand the options and reach the official source. We do not aim to break urgent news or replace official bodies.

## Choosing topics

We choose a topic based on:

- Recurring questions from students and families.
- Confusion or outdated information in circulation.
- A need for a security or procedural explanation.
- A gap in the available Arabic content.

We do not publish a topic merely to fill a calendar or attract visits. If there is not enough information, we delay publication or state the limits.

## Ordering sources

We rank sources by relevance and reliability:

1. **Primary official sources:** the ministry, academies, schools and official announcements.
2. **Official documents:** published guides and procedures.
3. **Reliable secondary sources:** known media or educational institutions, with the original checked.
4. **Field experience:** useful for context, but not a substitute for an official source.

When information is sensitive or liable to change, we name the source and the verification date. If we cannot find a source, we do not present a guess as fact.

## Verification

We ask basic questions:

- Who is behind the information?
- What is its date?
- Is the link original?
- Does the text say what it is claimed to say?
- Are there independent sources?
- Does it conflict with an official source?

We do not rely on a screenshot alone when the original link is available. We do not publish "leaks" as official results.

## Review and updates

We review pages when:

- A procedure or official link changes.
- A report of an error arrives.
- The school year or context changes.
- A gap in the explanation is discovered.

The last update date appears on the page. Updating the date does not mean every paragraph changed, only that the page was reviewed.

## Automated content

We may use automated tools to help organise, draft or proofread. We do not publish automated content without editorial review, and we do not attribute it to an invented person or expert. Editorial responsibility remains with the site team.

## Independence and conflicts

The site is independent of the ministry and official bodies. We do not sell account services or results. If we add advertising or partnerships in future, we will say so and separate editorial content from advertising.

We do not publish paid content that looks like a normal article without a clear label.

## Authors

We use the identity "Massar Services editorial team" rather than invented names. We do not attribute an article to someone who did not write it, and we do not display qualifications or titles that are not real. If a specialist reviews content, we say so where possible.

## Corrections

We welcome corrections. See the [corrections policy](/en/corrections-policy/) to learn how to report an issue and what happens next. We correct clear errors and explain the change when it is significant.

## What we do not claim

- We do not claim to be an official body.
- We do not claim that every statement is final.
- We do not claim to cover every case.
- We do not claim to provide legal advice.
- We do not claim to have tested every link from every country.

## Contact

For any editorial question or suggestion, use the [contact page](/en/contact-us/). Do not send your account details.
