## Before you send

This form is for improving the content:

- Reporting outdated or incorrect information.
- Reporting a broken link.
- Suggesting a topic or a new guide.
- Asking about the site's content or policies.
- Reporting an accessibility problem.

If your question concerns your Massar account, grades or result, the right channel is the official platform or your school. We cannot access your account or change your data.

## What we can do

- Review information we published.
- Correct an editorial error or a link.
- Clarify a source or an update date.
- Consider a topic suggestion.
- Improve accessibility where possible.

## What we cannot do

- Recover or change a password.
- Activate an account or fix permissions.
- Display grades or results.
- Issue an official document.
- Influence an administrative decision.
- Provide legal advice.

## Tips for your message

Use a clear subject, include the page address, describe the statement you believe is wrong, and cite the source that supports the correction. Do not send personal information we do not need. If the message concerns a student, do not include their full name or sensitive details.

> [!SECURITY] Do not send login details
> Never share your password, verification code or Massar code with anyone, including this site's team. We do not ask for them and do not need them.

## Direct email

You can also write to the email address shown below the form. Use a mailbox you can access, because we may ask for clarification before making a correction.
