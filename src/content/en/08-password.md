## Before you start

Recovery depends on what the official platform offers and what your school allows. There is no single guaranteed method for every case. Start by checking three things:

1. Are you using the right space: Moutamadris, Waliye or Moudaris?
2. Is your username correct?
3. Is there a "forgot password" option on the official login page?

If the option is missing, the solution is not necessarily impossible. It means you need another channel, usually the school.

## The official route

1. Open the official login page for the correct space.
2. Look for the password recovery or "forgot password" link.
3. Follow the steps shown, and enter only what the official platform requests.
4. Never send a verification code to anyone, even someone claiming to be support.
5. After changing the password, sign out of other devices if that option exists.

If a message does not arrive, check your spam folder if email is used, or check the details on record. Do not request it dozens of times, as this may trigger temporary restrictions.

## When self-service is unavailable

Self-service recovery may not be enabled for every account. In that case:

- Contact the school or the relevant administration.
- Explain that you cannot sign in, and state your role and the space involved.
- Do not send your old or new password in an ordinary message.
- Follow the identity verification procedure set by the school.

You may need to attend in person or provide a document, depending on the school. That is a protection measure, not unnecessary bureaucracy.

## What not to do

- **Do not share the account** with someone who offers to fix it.
- **Do not pay** a "Massar recovery" or "code generator" website.
- **Do not install** unofficial apps that ask for login details.
- **Do not send** identity documents or enrolment papers to an unknown party.
- **Do not try** random passwords for a long period.
- **Do not reuse** the same password on other accounts.

> [!WARNING] Phishing messages
> You may receive a message claiming that your account will be closed or that your grades are ready, with a login link. Do not click it. Open the official website manually and check.

## If the account was compromised

If your password or account details changed without your knowledge:

1. Try to change the password through the official channel immediately.
2. Inform the school or the relevant administration.
3. Review the devices you used, and look for suspicious apps or extensions.
4. Do not share the details of the incident in public groups.

## A stronger password

- Use a long passphrase that is easy for you to remember and hard to guess.
- Avoid birthdays, phone numbers or a student's name.
- Do not reuse the same password on other websites.
- Use a reputable password manager if possible.
- Enable two-step verification if the platform supports it.

## Summary

Password recovery goes through the official platform or the school, not this website. Do not pay middlemen, do not share verification codes, and act quickly if you suspect unauthorised access.
