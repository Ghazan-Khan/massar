## First: identify the right space

Before entering any details, ask: who am I in this process?

- **Student:** normally uses Massar Moutamadris.
- **Parent or guardian:** normally uses Massar Waliye.
- **Teacher or administrator:** uses Massar Moudaris or the portal specified by their school.

Using the wrong space is one of the most common causes of failure. Moutamadris credentials do not automatically work in Waliye, and the reverse is also true.

## Official addresses

| Space | Official address |
|---|---|
| Massar Moutamadris | https://massarservice.men.gov.ma/moutamadris/Account |
| Massar Waliye | https://massarservice.men.gov.ma/waliye/Account |
| Massar portal | https://massar.men.gov.ma |

For Moudaris, the exact entry point may vary by school. Use the address provided by your administration and confirm that it belongs to an official domain.

## Pre-login checklist

1. **Address:** are you on the official domain? Do not sign in from a shortened link or advertisement.
2. **Space:** are you trying Moutamadris, Waliye or Moudaris?
3. **Username:** watch for upper and lower case, and accidental spaces.
4. **Password:** check the keyboard language and whether Caps Lock is on.
5. **Browser:** update it or try another one, and disable suspicious extensions.
6. **Connection:** try another network if possible, and avoid public networks when entering a password.

## Sign-in steps

1. Open the official address directly.
2. Enter your details manually instead of relying on an old autofill entry.
3. Complete any verification step that appears.
4. After signing in, check the name or school to confirm you are in the right account.
5. If an error appears, note the message and avoid dozens of repeated attempts.

## Reading an error message

There is no universal list of messages, but errors generally fall into these groups:

- **Incorrect details:** check your typing, then use recovery if available.
- **Inactive or suspended account:** contact the school or administration.
- **Service error:** it may be temporary. Try later or check for an official notice.
- **Insufficient permission:** you may be in the wrong space or need access from an administrator.

Do not assume that a general message means your account was deleted. Gather the facts and ask the competent authority.

> [!SECURITY] Do not send screenshots containing your details
> When asking for help, do not send your password, one-time code or a full screenshot of the account. Send the error text only, with personal information hidden.

## Common mistakes

- Using a child's details to enter Waliye.
- Assuming the Massar Code is the password.
- Copying a password with an invisible space.
- Using an old link from a search engine.
- Trying to sign in through an unofficial app.
- Sharing the account with someone who says they will "fix" it.

## When to contact the school

Contact the school or relevant administration if:

- You have confirmed the space, address and details, but login still fails.
- A message refers to an account or permission problem.
- The account is new and has not been activated.
- Your details changed without your knowledge.

## Summary

Most login problems are solved by choosing the right space, checking the details and using the official address. Never share your password, never pay a middleman, and contact the school if the problem continues.
