## Our commitment

We want the content to be readable and usable by as many people as possible, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or text zoom. We target the four WCAG 2.2 principles: perceivable, operable, understandable and robust, at level AA.

This is a continuous improvement goal, not a claim of full conformance. No external audit certificate is currently published.

## What we have implemented

- Semantic HTML with a heading hierarchy and defined regions.
- A "skip to content" link at the start of the page.
- Keyboard navigation support and a visible focus indicator.
- Clear form labels and status messages through `aria-live`.
- Considered colour contrast, without relying on colour alone.
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` to reduce animation.
- Right-to-left Arabic and left-to-right English support.
- Text that can be enlarged without breaking the layout at common sizes.
- Text alternatives for decorative or functional images.
- A sitemap and search to make pages easier to find.
- Responsive design for phones and tablets.

## Known limitations

- Some pages contain tables or technical content that may be long on small screens.
- Some external links, especially official platforms, are outside our control for accessibility.
- Undiscovered errors may exist, particularly in new content.
- We have not yet run comprehensive testing with every screen reader and browser.
- We do not currently offer audio versions or sign language versions.

## Alternative content

If there is content you cannot access, you can contact us to request an alternative explanation or a different format. We will try to provide reasonable assistance, without promising a format we cannot produce.

## Reporting a barrier

When reporting, please include:

- The page address.
- The type of barrier, such as a keyboard, contrast or screen reader problem.
- The browser or assistive technology you use.
- What you tried to do and what happened.

Use the [contact page](/en/contact-us/) and choose a suitable subject. Do not send your account details.

## Response

We aim to acknowledge an accessibility report within 5 working days and review it within 10 working days where possible. Some fixes may take longer if they involve design or external content.

## Compatibility

The site works in modern browsers such as Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge. Some features may not work in very old browsers or in settings that block JavaScript. Interactive search needs JavaScript, but alternative links remain available without it.

## A note on language

The Arabic content is the primary version, and English is a parallel version. Some editorial details may differ between the two. If you find a conflict, rely on the most recent Arabic page or contact us for clarification.

## Summary

We are committed to continuous improvement and we acknowledge the limitations. If you meet a barrier, report it with clear details. Your help makes the site better for everyone.
