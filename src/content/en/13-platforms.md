## Why are there several platforms?

Morocco's education system uses several digital services. Some are connected with the school pathway, others with distance learning, exams or digital identity. Each service has its own address and purpose. Using the wrong one can make you think your account is broken.

## The main platforms

| Platform | General role | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Ministry of National Education | Institutional website and official announcements | First source for official notices |
| Massar Moutamadris | Student space | A student account |
| Massar Waliye | Parent and guardian space | A parent or guardian account |
| Massar portal | A portal connected with the system | May redirect to other services |
| Telmidtice | Learning resources and distance learning | Not the grades space |
| CNEE | Services connected with exams and evaluation | Check official announcements |
| Identity provider | A shared sign-in service linked to Massar | Never enter details on a copycat page |

This table is descriptive and brief. Service names, interfaces and sign-in methods may change. Rely on the official website and the announcement for the specific service.

## Massar versus Telmidtice

Massar is connected with the school pathway and administrative or educational records. Telmidtice is an education platform normally used for learning resources and distance learning. Do not look for grades on the lessons platform, and do not look for lessons in the grades space.

The platforms may complement each other, but that does not mean the account is the same. Check your school's instructions.

## How to verify an official link

1. Find the link from the Ministry website, not from an advertisement.
2. Check the domain: official addresses normally end in `men.gov.ma`.
3. Be careful with similar domains or spelling mistakes.
4. Do not enter a password on a page with strange ads or excessive requests for information.
5. If a link does not open from outside Morocco, do not look for an unofficial "mirror".

> [!OFFICIAL] The golden rule
> An official platform does not ask for your password through WhatsApp or email, and does not charge for a result or account recovery. When in doubt, stop and ask your school.

## Access problems

Some links may fail for several reasons:

- Temporary maintenance.
- Geographic restrictions or attack protection.
- A weak connection.
- An old browser.
- An outdated address.

These possibilities do not justify using an unofficial website. Wait or use another official channel, such as the school.

## What this website offers

We provide explanations and guidance, not account services. We cannot:

- Create or activate an account.
- Recover a password.
- Show grades or results.
- Edit a record in the system.
- Issue official documents.

## Summary

Official platforms are multiple, and each has a role. Start from the Ministry website, check the domain, and do not assume one account works everywhere. If access fails, return to the school rather than an alternative site.
