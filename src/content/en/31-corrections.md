## What do we correct?

We review reports concerning:

- An incorrect factual statement.
- A broken link or one that leads somewhere unexpected.
- An inaccurate date or platform name.
- A misleading or incomplete explanation.
- A language or formatting error that affects understanding.
- An accessibility problem.

We do not correct:

- Personal opinions not presented as facts.
- Requests to change a result or an official record.
- Administrative disputes unrelated to the content.
- Requests to remove content without a legitimate reason.

## How to report

Use the [contact page](/en/contact-us/) and choose the subject "correction" or "broken link". Include:

1. The page address or title.
2. The current text you believe is wrong.
3. The correction you propose.
4. The source supporting the correction, if available.
5. The date you noticed the issue.

Do not send your password, account details or unnecessary personal documents. If a document is necessary, redact sensitive details.

## What we do with a report

1. We receive and log the report.
2. We check the page and the source.
3. We ask for clarification if the report is insufficient.
4. We correct the page if the error is established.
5. We update the page date where needed.
6. We explain a significant correction on the page or in an editorial note.

## Timeframes

We aim to:

- Acknowledge a report within 5 working days.
- Review and reply within 10 working days where possible.
- Correct clear errors as soon as we can.

These are targets, not a legal guarantee. Review may take longer if an official source must be checked or if the report is complex.

## Types of correction

- **Correction:** changing a statement established as wrong.
- **Clarification:** adding context or explanation to prevent misunderstanding.
- **Update:** amending information that has changed over time.
- **Removal:** deleting content that cannot be verified or is no longer appropriate.

We may add a note below the page explaining the change, especially if it affects an earlier understanding.

## If we do not make a correction

We may decline a report if:

- There is not enough evidence.
- The request concerns an opinion rather than a fact.
- The purpose is to disrupt the site or abuse the team.
- The requested correction conflicts with a clearer official source.

We will try to explain why a correction was not made where possible.

## Transparency

We do not delete reports simply because they criticise us. We keep an internal review log and do not publish a reporter's details without consent. If a correction is significant, we explain it to readers.

## Contact

To report an error, use the [contact page](/en/contact-us/). Do not send login details. See also the [editorial policy](/en/editorial-policy/) for our approach to sources.
